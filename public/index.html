AQIverse brings current city air-quality observations into an interactive 3D globe.

LIVE DATA

Select a city to retrieve its latest available IQAir observation. Unloaded or unavailable locations show a dash. Coloured halos help distinguish loaded city points and do not represent measured pollution coverage.

How to explore

Search by country, state or province, city, or urban area to fly directly there at a useful zoom. Drag or use the arrow keys to rotate Earth. Scroll, pinch, double-tap, or use + and − for fast zoom up to 600%. At closer range, state and province boundaries, city AQI markers, and names appear. Press / to search, ? for help, or Space to play or pause while the globe is focused.

Connecting the real world

Readings use the IQAir API through a secure backend. Observations are cached for up to 30 minutes, unavailable values remain empty, and credentials stay on the server.

More local air

The globe includes 163 listed locations across Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, and Oceania. Select a marker to load its nearest available IQAir observation.
